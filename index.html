Account Deletion

Want to delete your BreakTheIce account?

We're sorry to see you go! Follow the simple steps below to permanently delete your account and all associated data.

📱 Delete Account Through the App:

  1. Open the BreakTheIce app
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Scroll down and tap "Account Settings"
  4. Tap "Delete Account"
  5. Confirm your decision by typing "DELETE"
  6. Your account will be permanently deleted within 24 hours

✉️ Delete Account by Email:

  1. Send an email to support@breaktheice.app
  2. Use subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  3. Include your registered email address
  4. We'll process your request within 48 hours
  5. You'll receive a confirmation email when completed

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

  • ✅ All your personal data is permanently removed
  • ✅ All chat history is deleted
  • ✅ Your email is removed from our systems
  • ✅ Any active subscription is cancelled
  • ✅ You'll receive a confirmation email

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you change your mind, you'll need to create a new account from scratch.
